# Risk Review Process

The sample uses a transparent 1-5 likelihood multiplied by 1-5 impact score. Gross exposure is the sum of current active scores; residual exposure uses the post-mitigation estimates.

At each risk review:

1. Confirm the description, cause, consequence and affected project.
2. Review gross likelihood and impact with supporting evidence.
3. Verify a named owner, mitigation and due date.
4. Challenge mitigation progress and dependencies.
5. Reassess residual likelihood and impact.
6. Escalate overdue or threshold-breaching items.
7. Record decisions, action owners and acceptance authority.

The scoring model is illustrative. An organization must define its own criteria, thresholds, aggregation rules and acceptance authorities.
