# Internal Audit Preparation Checklist

This is a generic preparation checklist, not a certified audit checklist or a substitute for competent independent auditors.

- Confirm audit objective, scope, criteria, schedule and responsible participants.
- Confirm auditor competence, independence and conflict-of-interest handling.
- Gather approved procedures, plans, records, prior findings and relevant customer requirements.
- Select samples with a documented rationale and preserve traceability to evidence.
- Record factual observations separately from conclusions.
- Classify findings using the organization's approved scheme.
- Link every required action to an owner, due date and verification method.
- Review correction, root cause, corrective action and effectiveness evidence.
- Obtain authorized closure and retain the resulting records.
- Feed recurring themes into management review, risk review and process improvement.
