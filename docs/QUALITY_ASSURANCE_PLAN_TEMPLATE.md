# Quality Assurance Plan Template

Use this outline as a review aid. Project stakeholders remain accountable for content, approval and change control.

## 1. Purpose and scope

Define the product, lifecycle boundaries, interfaces, exclusions and required deliverables.

## 2. Roles and approvals

Identify the customer, project manager, engineering leads, quality owner, independent reviewers and approval authority. Record deputies and escalation routes.

## 3. Applicable requirements

List contract, customer, statutory, regulatory and internal requirements with source, owner, verification method, evidence location and status.

## 4. Lifecycle reviews and gates

Define entry and exit criteria, required evidence, decision owners and action follow-up for initiation, design, implementation, verification, release and closure.

## 5. Complaints and corrective actions

Define intake, severity, containment, structured root-cause analysis, action ownership, effectiveness evidence, customer communication and closure authority.

## 6. Risk management

Define scoring, review cadence, escalation thresholds, mitigation ownership, due dates and residual-risk acceptance.

## 7. Audits and assessments

Define scope, competence and independence expectations, schedule, evidence, findings, corrective actions and closure verification.

## 8. KPIs and reporting

Define every measure's formula, source, owner, refresh cadence, target, escalation threshold and audience.

## 9. Documents and records

Define repositories, naming, versioning, review and approval, access, retention, protection and disposition.

## 10. Approval and change history

Record baseline, approvers, date, revision reason and impact assessment. Do not treat a generated summary as an approved plan.
